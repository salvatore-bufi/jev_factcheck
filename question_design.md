# Initial question design

This pack was written from the task specification and official documentation, without accessing benchmark examples. Each question evaluates the provided document and claim; none asks whether the claim is true in the outside world. The positive class means complete support, so contradiction and insufficient evidence both map to negative. A missing-detail question and a contradiction question provide complementary failure signals. The latter is diagnostic on its own: absence of contradiction does not imply support.

The seven questions compare short and explicit Noul phrasing, reversed polarity, binary and ternary Choice, an ordered support Score, and direct contradiction detection. The stricter rubrics distinguish faithful paraphrase from added facts, excessive specificity, and lost qualifications. Three fixed combinations test simple agreement rules without adding another model call. Avoid fitting arbitrary ensemble weights to a small development set.

The [API reference](https://docs.typesafe.ai/api) defines `noul` as the yes probability, Choice `probabilities` as an option distribution, and Score levels as zero-based indices. Extract the positive-class probability rather than `confidence`, which describes certainty about any answer. For the four-level Score, compare `score / 3` with `probabilities["3"]`; only its final rubric level denotes complete support. The JSON contains extraction metadata outside the API-ready `questions` map.

The [primitives guide](https://docs.typesafe.ai/primitives) says questions sharing a state are evaluated independently and may be batched. The [citation cookbook](https://docs.typesafe.ai/cookbooks/citation_check) motivates separating support, contradiction, and insufficient evidence, but its confidence threshold and synthetic example results are not benchmark calibration. Do not use confidence as a support score or copy its threshold.

Tune candidate selection and the binary decision threshold on development data, then freeze them before test evaluation. Record the resolved model version: `jev-latest` can change. The documented endpoint exposes model, state, and questions; it does not document temperature or top-p. Use a small prespecified candidate family and a held-out development check to limit selection overfit. No credentials or API calls were needed to author this pack.
