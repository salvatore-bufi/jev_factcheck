# One development-only revision

The first round evaluated 2,299 tuning rows. Best tuned source-macro balanced accuracy was 78.65% (binary Choice, threshold 0.68), compared with 75.27% for the direct-support question at 0.5. These figures are tuning estimates.

Fourteen randomly selected tuning errors (seed 55) suggested relationship errors involving time, proposals versus completed events, attribution, and unsupported detail. Some supported examples were also rejected despite ordinary paraphrase or transcript noise. Labels remain unchanged; several disagreements are ambiguous and should not be assumed to be model mistakes without adjudication.

Round two adds three questions: a concise entailment Noul, a faithful-statement binary Choice, and an atomic relationship-error Noul. Compare two predefined compositions. The relationship-error score is diagnostic only by itself. No further question revisions are permitted after this round. Use the top three candidates across both rounds as the fixed selection shortlist.

The selection split and test examples have not been inspected for this revision. Test data has not been downloaded.
